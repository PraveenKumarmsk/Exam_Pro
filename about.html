About ExamPro

A modern, honest practice platform for Indian competitive exams — SSC, Railway, Bihar exams, Banking and more.

Our mission

ExamPro exists to give every aspirant — in a metro or a small town — the same quality of practice: realistic timed tests, honest analytics and clear explanations, without a heavy price tag.

  • Demo questions are labelled as demo — they are never passed off as official papers.
  • Statistics on the home page come from what is actually on the site.
