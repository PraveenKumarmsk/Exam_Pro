Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions from aspirants.

Is ExamPro free to use?
Yes — the core mock tests, custom test builder and performance analysis are free. See pricing for the optional paid plans planned for later.
Which exams are covered?
SSC (CGL, CHSL, MTS, GD, CPO), Railway (NTPC, Group D, ALP, Technician), Bihar exams (BPSC, Police, BSSC, Daroga), Banking, UPSC, and select state and defence exams. Tests are being added exam by exam.
Can I build my own test?
Yes. Use the Custom Test Builder to pick subject, difficulty, number of questions, duration and language.
Are the questions official?
No. Questions on this site are demo practice questions and are labelled as such. Previous year papers will be added only when licensing is confirmed.
Where is my progress saved?
In this browser, on your device. You can export a backup from your profile page.
Do you support Hindi?
Hindi-subject questions are available now. Full bilingual translation of every question is planned.
