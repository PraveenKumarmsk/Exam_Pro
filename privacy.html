Privacy Policy

What ExamPro stores, where it lives, and how you stay in control of it.

What we store

In this development build, ExamPro runs entirely in your browser. The following is saved only on your own device (browser localStorage):

  • Your name, email, target exam and daily goal if you create a profile.
  • Your test attempts, answers, time spent per question, bookmarks and the list of questions you got wrong.
  • Your theme choice, text-size preference, selected plan, study-note progress and any current-affairs notes you write.

Your password is never stored or transmitted.

What we send to a server

Nothing. There is no backend connected yet, so no personal data leaves your device. If a backend is added later, this policy will be updated first to explain what is collected, why, and for how long.

Cookies and similar storage

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. We use browser storage (localStorage) to remember your progress and preferences. Clearing your browser data removes it.

Your control

  • Export a full backup from your profile page.
  • Delete your test history, or your whole profile and all data, from the same page.
  • Clearing site data in your browser also removes everything.

Children

ExamPro is intended for exam aspirants. Younger users should use it with a parent or guardian's guidance.

Changes and contact

We will update this page when the way data is handled changes. Questions? Use the contact page.
